Research Interests

My research focuses on visual representation learning for 3D/4D understanding and robotics. I am interested in multi-view learning and spatial representations that capture the geometry and semantics of the physical world. My interests also include compact 3D/4D reconstruction and world action models (WAMs), with the long-term goal of connecting visual perception with action.

  • Multi-view representation learning and 3D scene understanding
  • Compact 3D/4D representations and feed-forward scene reconstruction
  • World action models and visual representations for robotics
