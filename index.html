Research Interests

My research focuses on geometry-aware visual representations for 3D/4D understanding and robotics. I am particularly interested in lifting 2D foundation-model features into dense 3D representations while preserving semantic information and consistency across views. I also study compact 3D/4D reconstruction and efficient vision-language-action models, with the long-term goal of enabling robots to perceive and interact with the physical world.

  • Geometry-guided multi-view representations, dense correspondence, and 3D feature lifting
  • Compact 3D/4D representations and feed-forward scene reconstruction
  • Visual representations and efficient vision-language-action models for robotics
